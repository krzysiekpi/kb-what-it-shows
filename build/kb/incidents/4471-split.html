parser (ticket). The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out flipped. This is the bug the queued fix is for. It reaches people through the app, by email, and through booking-v2 forms.

hint (ticket). Only booking-v3 forms, and only three tenants. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type month first, as told, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The parser fix does not change this. The hint text or the form’s reading has to change.

daymove (ticket). A day off by one, with no month and day swapped. The desk had already listed these reports as a one-day move, but they were filed under parser by tenant. Different symptom, so a different bug until engineering shows otherwise. A time zone shift is the first thing to check: one report has the same meeting on two different days in two cities.

Why the merge hid it

Decision 0001 merges on subject. These reports share subjects such as “Booking on the wrong date” and “Date is wrong” across all three problems. Subject says what the reporter saw, not what broke. Triage also shows that tenant and channel decide the cause, and intake never looks them up. Intake’s own tag disagrees with the cause for a share of the reports, so it cannot be used to split them.

What to do

  • Mark #4471 as split. Keep it for parser only, because that is the bug the queued fix covers.
  • Move hint and daymove to their own tickets, linked in the table above.
  • Write a new decision record that replaces 0001 for incidents. Do not edit 0001: it is accepted.
